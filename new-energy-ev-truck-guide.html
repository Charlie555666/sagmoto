2. i9 — Heavy Electric Platform

The i9 is SAGMOTO's heavy EV platform for closed-loop heavy duty: container drayage in ports, mine development hauling, and heavy urban distribution. Its 350 kW-class permanent-magnet motors deliver diesel-class torque from zero rpm, and LFP battery chemistry is chosen for cycle life and thermal stability in hot climates. Details: i9 electric truck deep dive.

3. i5 — The Urban EV Workhorse

The i5 is where EV economics already beat diesel today. As a box truck, refrigerator truck or sanitation chassis, the i5 covers typical 150-250 km urban duty with overnight AC charging and regenerative braking in traffic. Field notes: urban logistics application, cold-chain application, municipal sanitation application, and the i5 vs Volvo FE Electric comparison.

4. E9 EV — Light Electric Distribution

The E9 EV brings the same LFP battery and motor strategy to the light segment — inner-city distribution, campus logistics and municipal fleets. It shares cab and body options with the diesel E9, letting mixed fleets keep a single parts catalogue. Comparison: E9 EV vs E9 diesel.

5. Charging Strategy & Infrastructure

  • Single-shift city duty — AC 22 kW overnight: 3-5 h full charge, lowest infrastructure cost (a 3-phase socket per truck).
  • Multi-shift fleets — DC 120 kW fast: 80% in ~1 h between shifts.
  • Weakest-grid sites — solar + storage depot combinations; we provide the depot sizing worksheet with every order.

Climate notes: LFP chemistry tolerates 45C+ ambient with liquid-cooled packs on i9; cold-climate battery care in winter charging guide.

6. TCO vs Diesel

At 200 km/day, typical break-even is 2.5-4 years: energy cost per km drops 40-60% versus diesel (grid-dependent), and routine maintenance falls with no oil, filters or turbo. Residual battery value after 3,000+ cycles supports second-life storage. The main watch items: tyre wear from torque (manageable with eco-mode), and charging discipline.

7. FAQ

How far on one charge?

i5: 200-270 km; i9: 250-350 km; E9 EV: 180-230 km — depending on body, load and route profile.

What charging do I need?

AC 22 kW overnight covers single-shift; DC 120 kW fast charging for multi-shift fleets. Depot sizing worksheet provided with every order.

Are EVs viable in my market?

Where diesel is expensive or unreliable and duty cycles are predictable: city logistics, ports, airports, mining camps, closed industrial loops. Open long-haul remains diesel.

TCO vs diesel?

Typical break-even at 200 km/day is 2.5-4 years on fuel and maintenance savings, before incentives.
