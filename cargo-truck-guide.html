2. E3 MAX — National Distribution Workhorse

The E3 MAX is the volume cargo platform for breweries, FMCG and building-material distribution. With the Yuchai YC6MK up to 400 HP, Fast 12JSD200T and a 10-16 t payload envelope, it balances purchase price against parts availability better than any truck in its class. Field data: beverage distribution application, cold-chain buying guide, Philippines food logistics.

3. E9 & X9 — Urban and Last-Mile

The E9 covers urban and rural distribution up to 10 t; the X9 is the inner-city specialist for 3-7 t multi-drop work with a tight turning circle. Both are available as box, curtain-side and refrigerated bodies. Applications: Middle East urban logistics, Africa rural last-mile.

4. X3s — Long-Haul Cargo

For 500 km+ palletised runs, the X3s 6x4 cargo truck with Cummins ISME 420 HP shares the X3000 tractor's proven powertrain in a cargo format — the same 12JSD200T, the same parts catalog, trained driver familiarity across your fleet. Background: X3s Southeast Asia market review.
