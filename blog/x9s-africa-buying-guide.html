Import Procedures by Country

DRC (Democratic Republic of Congo)

The DRC is Africa's largest copper and cobalt producer, with mining operations in the Katanga (Lubumbashi) and Lualaba (Kolwezi) provinces. Import procedures include:

  1. Import permit from the Ministry of Mines (for mining equipment)
  2. Customs clearance at Dar es Salaam (Tanzania) or Durban (South Africa) port, then transit to DRC
  3. Customs duty: 5 percent for mining equipment (negotiated with mining authority)
  4. VAT: 16 percent
  5. Vehicle registration with the mining authority for mine-site operation

Transit from Dar es Salaam to Lubumbashi is approximately 2,200 km by road, taking 10-14 days including border crossings at Tanzania-Zambia (Nakonde) and Zambia-DRC (Kasumbalesa). Alternatively, trucks can be shipped to Durban and transported by road through Botswana, Zambia, and into DRC (3,500 km, 14-18 days).

Zambia

Zambia's copper belt (Kitwe, Ndola, Chingola) is the second-largest copper mining region in Africa. Import procedures are straightforward:

  1. Customs clearance at Dar es Salaam or Durban port
  2. ZRA (Zambia Revenue Authority) customs declaration
  3. Customs duty: 15 percent for commercial vehicles
  4. VAT: 16 percent
  5. RTSA (Road Transport and Safety Agency) vehicle registration

Ghana

Ghana's gold mining sector (Obuasi, Tarkwa, Ahafo) operates approximately 2,000-3,000 heavy-duty trucks. Import is via Tema port:

  1. Ghana Standards Authority import permit
  2. Customs duty: 20 percent for commercial vehicles
  3. VAT: 15 percent (plus 2.5 percent NHIL, 2.5 percent GETFL)
  4. DVLA (Driver and Vehicle Licensing Authority) registration

Guinea

Guinea's bauxite mining sector (Boke, Sangaredi) is one of the world's largest, with growing demand for heavy-duty trucks. Import via Conakry port:

  1. Ministry of Mines import permit (for mining equipment)
  2. Customs duty: 5 percent for mining equipment, 20 percent for general commercial
  3. VAT: 18 percent
  4. Vehicle registration with Ministry of Transport

Spare Parts Strategy for African Mining

In African mining operations, parts availability is the single most critical factor for truck availability. A truck waiting 2-3 weeks for a parts shipment from China loses USD 8,000-12,000 per week in lost production. A properly stocked parts depot can reduce downtime by 60-70 percent and improve truck availability from 75 percent to 88+ percent.

Shaanxi Fenghan Trading recommends the following spare parts strategy for African mining operations:

Initial Parts Package (Year 1)

  • Engine: filters (20 sets), belts (5 sets), turbocharger (1 unit), injectors (1 set)
  • Transmission: oil (200 L), clutch assembly (1 set), synchronizer components (1 set)
  • Brakes: lining (4 sets per axle), air dryer cartridges (4 units), brake valves (1 set)
  • Suspension: U-bolts (1 set), leaf springs (1 set per axle), shock absorbers (2 sets)
