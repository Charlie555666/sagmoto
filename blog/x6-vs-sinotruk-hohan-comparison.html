SAGMOTO X6 vs Sinotruk Hohan: Heavy-Duty Tractor Truck Comparison 2026

2026-09-11SAGMOTO BlogShaanxi Fenghan Trading Co., Ltd.

Introduction to the Comparison

The SAGMOTO X6 and Sinotruk Hohan represent two of the most widely exported Chinese heavy-duty tractor trucks in the 6x4, 430-440 HP class. Both trucks target the same market segments: long-haul freight, construction material transport, and heavy-duty logistics in Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia, and Southeast Asia. This comparison provides fleet operators with a detailed technical and economic analysis to inform procurement decisions. For a broader comparison of SAGMOTO dump truck models 6x4 8x4, our product page covers the full range.

While both trucks originate from Chinese manufacturers and share similar GVW ratings (49-55 tonnes), their engineering philosophies differ significantly. The SAGMOTO X6 uses a Cummins ISM11E5 engine with a FAST 16-speed transmission, prioritizing proven global component reliability. The Sinotruk Hohan uses a MAN-licensed D20 engine with a Sinotruk HW transmission, prioritizing technology transfer and integrated drivetrain design.

The Cummins ISM11E5 engine in the X6 benefits from Cummins' global service network, with genuine parts available in over 190 countries. This is a significant advantage for fleet operators in remote locations where the local dealer network for Chinese-brand engines may be thin. The MAN-licensed D20 in the Hohan offers tighter integration with the Sinotruk drivetrain but relies on Sinotruk's dealer network for parts supply, which is strong in China and growing in export markets but less established than Cummins' global footprint.

Torque and Gradeability Analysis

The X6's ISM11E5 produces 2,100 Nm versus the Hohan's 2,000 Nm, a 5 percent advantage. Combined with the FAST 16-speed transmission's lower first-gear ratio (13.80:1 vs 12.42:1 for the HW transmission), the X6 achieves a starting gradeability of 32 percent with a 49-tonne combination, compared to 28 percent for the Hohan. This difference is meaningful for mining and construction applications where steep ramp starts with full loads are routine.

The FAST 16-speed transmission provides 16 gear ratios through a combination of a main gearbox (8 gears) and a splitter (high/low), enabling the driver to match engine speed to road speed with greater precision. This is particularly advantageous in mixed terrain operations where frequent grade changes require gear splits to maintain engine rpm in the optimal torque band. The Sinotruk HW 10-speed transmission offers fewer gear steps but benefits from tighter integration with the D20 engine's torque curve, and its simpler design reduces driver training requirements.

The X6's double-layer frame provides 40 percent greater torsional stiffness than the Hohan's single-layer frame, translating to better frame durability in rough-road operations. The full air suspension on the X6's rear axle provides a constant frame height regardless of load, improving fifth-wheel coupling consistency and driver comfort. The Hohan's leaf spring with air helper offers a simpler, lower-maintenance suspension but lacks the load-height compensation of full air suspension.

Fuel Economy Comparison

In fleet testing across 50,000 km of mixed highway and urban operations with 40-tonne trailers, the SAGMOTO X6 recorded 34-38 litres per 100 km, while the Sinotruk Hohan recorded 36-40 litres per 100 km. The X6's 2 L/100km advantage is attributed to the Cummins ISM11E5's higher thermal efficiency (43 percent vs 41 percent) and the FAST 16-speed's closer gear ratios enabling lower engine rpm at cruise speed.

At an annual 120,000 km, the 2 L/100km fuel advantage saves approximately 2,400 litres of diesel per truck. At a diesel price of USD 1.20 per litre (typical for African and Middle Eastern markets), this represents annual savings of USD 2,880 per truck. For a 20-truck fleet, the annual fuel savings amount to USD 57,600, which exceeds the price premium of the X6 over the Hohan within the first year of operation.

Conclusion

The SAGMOTO X6 and Sinotruk Hohan are both capable heavy-duty tractor trucks that serve the export market effectively. The X6's advantages lie in its Cummins powertrain with global parts support, double-layer frame for rough-road durability, full air suspension, and superior fuel economy. The Hohan's advantages lie in its lower purchase price, lower maintenance cost, and the integrated MAN-technology drivetrain. For fleet operators in markets with strong Sinotruk dealer networks, the Hohan offers compelling value. For operators prioritizing global parts availability, fuel economy, and chassis durability in challenging road conditions, the SAGMOTO X6 delivers a stronger long-term proposition.
