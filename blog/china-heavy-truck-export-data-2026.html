China Heavy Truck Export Data 2026: H1 Volumes, Market Share and Top Destinations

2026-09-28Market Data9 min read

Quick answer: China's commercial vehicle exports reached 664,500 units in H1 2026, up 32.58% year-on-year. In the heavy-truck segment, Sinotruk exported 108,351 units (+57.1%) — nearly half of China's heavy-truck exports — while Shaanxi Automobile (SHACMAN/SAGMOTO) exported 36,000 units (+34.2%). The fastest-growing destination regions in H1 2026 were Africa (+66%), Southeast Asia (+61%), Latin America (+75%) and Asia-Oceania (+90%).

What Does This Data Mean for an Overseas Buyer?

Three practical takeaways:

  1. Lead times are lengthening. With export volumes up 30-57% at the two heavy-truck leaders, popular export specs (6x4 and 8x4 tippers, 380-430 HP) regularly carry 30-60 day production queues. Book build slots before your project's procurement window, not after.

Q: How many heavy trucks did China export in the first half of 2026?

Q: Which Chinese company exports the most heavy trucks?

Q: Which regions are buying the most Chinese trucks in 2026?
