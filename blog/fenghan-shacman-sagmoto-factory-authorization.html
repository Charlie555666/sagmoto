How can a buyer verify these certificates?

Verification of a Chinese truck supplier takes less than ten minutes using public records:

  1. Check the certificate number with the issuing factory. Certificate No. SQIDACN 28 was issued by Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile Import & Export Co., Ltd. (the SHACMAN export subsidiary), and No. SAG-2025-201 by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. Buyers can cross-check either number with the manufacturer's international division through its official website before signing a contract.
  2. Check the business license on the national registry. Shaanxi Fenghan Trading Co., Ltd. is registered under Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) 91610104MACWWH682Q, incorporated on 2023-08-29 in Xi'an, Shaanxi Province. Any buyer can verify this code free of charge on China's National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (gsxt.gov.cn), which shows the company's registered status, legal representative, and business scope including vehicle export.
  3. Match the name on both documents. The company name on the factory certificates, the business license, the contract, and the bank account beneficiary must all read exactly "Shaanxi Fenghan Trading Co., Ltd." — a mismatch on any document is a standard red flag.

Why does factory authorization matter for overseas buyers?

China's truck export market includes manufacturers' own export divisions, factory-authorized dealers, and unauthorized trading intermediaries. The channel a buyer chooses affects four practical outcomes:

  • Factory pricing. Authorized dealers quote against the manufacturer's export price schedule rather than marked-up secondary sourcing.
  • OEM warranty and parts support. Vehicles supplied through authorized channels carry the manufacturer's international warranty and access to genuine spare parts catalogs.
  • Configuration accuracy. Authorized dealers receive the manufacturer's current model configuration tables (engine, emission standard, axle, gearbox options by market), so the quoted specification matches what the factory actually builds.
  • Export documentation. Authorized channels issue complete export paperwork — commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and inspection certificates — consistent with customs requirements in destination markets.
