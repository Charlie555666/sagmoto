SAGMOTO Z3 Buying Guide for India: Specifying the 520HP Cummins M13 Prime Mover

October 12, 2026Buying/Import Guide14 min read

The Z3 tractor truck 520HP Cummins M13 is SAGMOTO's premium Indian-market prime mover, pairing a globally familiar Cummins M13 engine with a heavy 6x4 chassis for 49-tonne GCW bulk and container duty. For Indian fleet buyers who want European-class power with Chinese price discipline, the Z3 is the brand's flagship tractor. This buying guide covers the India specification.

Step 1: Confirm the Duty Cycle

The Z3 6x4 is rated to 49-tonne GCW under CMVR for a 3-axle trailer. It suits container corridors (Mumbai–Pune, Chennai–Bengaluru), steel and cement bulk, and cross-state agricultural haulage. For single-trailer 49t work the 520 HP M13 is ample; for hilly routes (e.g., the Western Ghats) specify the higher rear-axle ratio and the engine brake.

Step 2: BS-VI and CMVR Compliance

Every Z3 for India is BS-VI with SCR and on-board diagnostics. Under CMVR the 6x4 tractor with 3-axle trailer is capped at 49t GCW, so the 16-tonne-rated rear axle and correct load distribution are specified for legal operation. RHD is standard, with left-side driver ergonomics and a mirrored dashboard loom. Fenghan Trade supplies a BS-VI compliance dossier and supports state RTO registration.

Step 3: Cab and Driver Comfort

The Z3 uses a wide-body sleeper cab with air-suspended seat, flat floor and a 600 mm bunk for two-driver long-haul. For the Indian operator running 14–18 hour shifts, the sleeper and the AMT-like FAST 12-speed (with crawler and retarder) reduce driver fatigue on the long corridors.

Buying insight: The Z3 520HP at ₹45–52 lakh undercuts a European 520HP prime mover by 25–35 percent while matching GCW and power. The Cummins M13 commonality keeps workshop-training cost low, and the parts consignment at Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad and Chennai caps downtime.

Step 4: Pricing and Lead Time

2026 FOB pricing for the Z3 6x4 tractor is USD 46,000–56,000; landed ex-showroom-equivalent in India is roughly ₹45–52 lakh. Lead time is 55–80 days for stock tractors and 80–100 days for custom sleeper/ratio configurations. Fleet orders of 10+ units earn 3–8 percent volume discounts. Conclusion

The SAGMOTO Z3 is a well-specified, BS-VI-ready 520HP prime mover for the Indian 49t corridor in 2026.
