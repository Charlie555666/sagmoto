Import Procedures by GCC Country

Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is the largest GCC truck market, importing approximately 20,000-25,000 heavy-duty trucks annually. Import duty under the GCC Customs Union is 5 percent for commercial vehicles. The Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization (SASO) requires:

  1. SASO Certificate of Conformity (CoC) issued by an approved certification body
  2. Saudi emission compliance (equivalent to Euro V or higher)
  3. Vehicle inspection at Saudi Ports Authority (Mawani) upon arrival
  4. Registration with the Traffic Department (Moroor)
  5. Commercial registration and operating permit from Ministry of Transport

The SASO CoC process requires pre-shipment inspection and documentation review, typically taking 7-10 days. A Certificate of Origin (CO) from the China Council for the Promotion of International Trade (CCPIT) is required for preferential duty rates.

UAE

The UAE imports approximately 8,000-12,000 heavy-duty trucks annually. Import duty is 5 percent under GCC. The Emirates Authority for Standardization and Metrology (ESMA) requires ECAS (Emirates Conformity Assessment Scheme) certification. The UAE has the simplest import process in the GCC, with most trucks clearing Jebel Ali or Khalifa Port within 3-5 days of vessel arrival.

Qatar

Qatar imports 3,000-5,000 heavy-duty trucks annually, driven by ongoing infrastructure development ahead of major events and urban expansion. Import duty is 5 percent under GCC. Qatar requires Qatar General Organization for Standards and Metrology (QGOSM) certification. The process is similar to Saudi Arabia but typically faster (5-7 days).

Tipper Body Options

The X6 chassis supports various tipper body configurations for Middle East construction applications:

  • U-type rock body: 16 m3 capacity, Hardox 450 wear steel, 6mm floor / 5mm sides. For rock and aggregate haulage in quarry operations. Body weight: 4,500 kg. Cost: USD 8,000-11,000.
  • Flat body (square): 18 m3 capacity, Q345 steel, 8mm floor / 6mm sides. For sand, soil, and general construction material. Body weight: 4,200 kg. Cost: USD 6,500-8,500.
  • Semi-circular body: 15 m3 capacity, Hardox 450, for sticky materials (clay, wet soil). Body weight: 4,800 kg. Cost: USD 9,000-12,000.
  • 3-way tipper: 12 m3 capacity, Q345, for road construction and municipal work. Body weight: 4,000 kg. Cost: USD 7,000-9,500.
