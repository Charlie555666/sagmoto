X7 for Bangladesh Specifications

The X7 for Bangladesh uses the Yuchai YC4E / YC4D (160–240 HP, 600–900 Nm) with a FAST 6-speed. GVW spans 7,500–18,000 kg on a 3,600–6,100 mm wheelbase. Regulatory Context: BRTA and BNBC

Imported trucks must clear BRTA (Bangladesh Road Transport Authority) registration with RHD configuration, correct GVW plates, and emission compliance. The BNBC seismic and safety codes affect body construction for government and municipal contracts. Fenghan Trade supplies a BRTA-ready dossier and partners with Chittagong and Mongla customs brokers for CBU clearance. The 2026 duty environment favours consolidated shipments to control landed cost.

Second, cold-chain for fish, pharma and agri produce is growing with reefer bodies on the 12-tonne chassis. Third, city corporations are procuring refuse compactors and water jets on the 14–18-tonne X7 for municipal sanitation.
