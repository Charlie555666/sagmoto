Authorized SHACMAN Exporters in China (2026): How to Verify the Certificate Before You Pay

Quick answer: there is no official public list of authorized SHACMAN exporters — Shaanxi Automobile Group verifies authorizations company by company. A "verified" exporter is one whose certificate you can check: a certificate number (for example SQIDACN 28), an issuing factory entity, validity dates, and a company name that matches its business licence (USCC) and bank account. Any supplier who says "authorized" but cannot produce those four elements is making a marketing claim, not showing a credential.

This guide gives you the verification framework the factory itself uses, with a real certificate as the worked example, so you can check any name on any list — including us.

Is There an Official List of Authorized SHACMAN Exporters?

No — and that is exactly why buyers get burned. Shaanxi Automobile Group sells overseas through two structures:

  • The factory's own export arm (Shaanxi Heavy Duty Automobile Import & Export Co., Ltd. — "SHACMAN International"), built for government tenders and large fleet deals.
  • Factory-authorized trading companies, each holding a signed dealer certificate with a unique number, validity period and authorized scope. This is the channel that handles single-unit and small-fleet orders, mixed loading with spare parts, and fast communication.

Neither structure publishes a complete directory. So the practical question is not "who is on the list" — it is "can this specific company's certificate survive a 10-minute check?"

How Do You Verify an Exporter in 4 Steps?

  1. Get the certificate in writing. Ask for a scan showing the number, issuing entity, validity and scope. Refusal or a blurred image ends the conversation.
  2. Check the company on the national registry. Take the Unified Social Credit Code (18 characters) and search gsxt.gov.cn — China's official enterprise registry. The registered name, legal representative and "active" status must match the certificate exactly.
  3. Confirm with the factory. Email or call SHACMAN's export arm (contact via the official site global.shacman.com) and quote the certificate number. Factories confirm their own certificates — this is routine.
  4. Match the money trail. The proforma invoice and receiving bank account must be in the certificate holder's exact registered name. A personal account or a different company name is the single most reliable fraud signal.

Authorized Exporter vs Marketplace Trader: What Does the Channel Actually Buy You?

  • Factory warranty that answers. Authorized channel orders enter the factory warranty system; grey-channel trucks do not.
  • Genuine parts pipeline. We stock OE parts with factory part numbers in our online parts store and air-freight urgent items in 3-7 days.

  • Spec protection. An authorized exporter's job is to stop you mis-specifying (axles, body steel, emission level) — a trader's job is to sell what is in stock.
