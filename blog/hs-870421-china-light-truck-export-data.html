HS 870421 China Light Truck Export Data by Destination

2026-11-10Buyer Decision9 min read

Quick answer: In 2024 China exported roughly 518,400 diesel light trucks under HS 870421 (GVW up to 5 t), worth about 6.55 billion dollars at a 12,600 dollar average price. How Large Is China's HS 870421 Light Truck Export Market

HS 870421 covers diesel powered goods vehicles with a gross vehicle weight not exceeding 5 tonnes, a category that includes chassis cab, box, and dropside light trucks built for urban freight and last mile distribution. In 2024 China's exports under this code reached roughly 518,400 units, a substantial volume that confirms the country's role as the dominant low cost supplier of compact commercial vehicles to emerging and developing markets. The aggregate customs value of these shipments was about 6.55 billion US dollars, implying an average transaction value near 12,600 dollars per unit once the full range of basic chassis cab and fully built box bodies is blended. These aggregates are approximate customs statistics drawn from trade data and should be read as orders of magnitude rather than audited company accounts, but the relative ranking between destinations is stable across reporting sources. Buyers should note that the code captures only completed vehicles and knock down kits recorded as goods vehicles, so related parts and completely knocked down packs classified elsewhere are excluded from these totals, which means the figures slightly understate the true scale of Chinese light truck presence abroad.

Which Destinations Import the Most Chinese Light Trucks Under 5 Tonnes

The destination mix is concentrated but spread across several regions, with Latin America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Africa all taking meaningful volume. Mexico stands clearly first, reflecting near shore assembly, distributor networks, and replacement demand in a large domestic freight market. Chile and Peru follow as established South American importers with mature dealer service, while Vietnam and the Philippines anchor Southeast Asian demand. The Middle East takes a steady flow into Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates, and Africa appears both directly through South Africa and indirectly through the large undefined remainder that captures many smaller sub Saharan importers. Mexico Sets the Pace

Mexico alone took about 88,100 units, a 17 percent share, meaning roughly one in six Chinese HS 870421 trucks exported in 2024 went to that single market.

What Does the Average Unit Price Reveal About Market Positioning

The average unit price of about 12,600 dollars hides a wide spread that tells a story about how Chinese light trucks are positioned by destination. Mexico, at roughly 14,500 dollars per unit, absorbs higher specification vehicles with emissions equipment and creature comforts suited to a stricter regulatory and buyer environment. The Middle East sits in a similar 13,500 to 13,700 dollar band, reflecting both specifications and the preference for factory built bodies. Southeast Asia clusters around 12,200 to 12,900 dollars, a competitive mid band. Africa sits at the bottom, with South Africa near 11,200 dollars and the broad remainder near 10,900 dollars, because many shipments are bare chassis cab units destined for local body building rather than finished trucks, and price sensitivity is acute. A buyer reading the average therefore learns less than a buyer reading the destination specific price, which signals whether a market favours finished or knock down units.

How Has Export Growth Shifted Across Regions

Growth rates diverged sharply in 2024 and reveal where Chinese light truck expansion is now concentrated. Vietnam led with 22 percent year on year growth, a sign of distributor build out and replacement of aging fleets with affordable new units. South Africa followed at 17 percent, confirming sub Saharan demand for low cost freight vehicles despite a tougher macro backdrop. Mexico's 14 percent and the Others bucket's 13 percent show that the established leaders and the long tail of smaller markets both still expanded. By contrast Russia contracted 4 percent, the only named destination to decline, as sanctions, logistics friction, and local assembly shifts redirected flow. The Middle East grew modestly at 5 to 9 percent, a mature base adding volume without the explosive rates of newer entrants. Why Does the Destination Mix Matter for Buyers and Distributors

The destination mix matters because it predicts where spare parts, local body building, and after sales support will be strongest. A market like Mexico or Chile with high volume and a stable dealer base means a new distributor can expect training, warranty infrastructure, and a second hand resale channel that protects residual value. A fast growing market like Vietnam or South Africa signals opportunity but also the risk of oversupply and price erosion if too many importers enter at once. The low price African bucket warns that many units land as bare chassis cab, so a buyer must budget separately for local body work rather than assuming a turnkey truck. Reading the mix also helps a distributor choose a specification tier, since what sells in Mexico at 14,500 dollars will not clear at the 10,900 dollar African average without stripping equipment.

How Should a Buyer Use This Export Data When Sourcing

A buyer should treat these aggregates as a market map, not a price list, and then verify the specific supplier against it. High volume destinations indicate mature support networks worth preferring, while the average unit price per region sets a realistic budget ceiling for a given specification. Before committing, a buyer should confirm any supplier's credentials through authorized SAGMOTO distributor verification and review the SAGMOTO cargo truck flatbed box stake range when matching a chassis to a body requirement, because the export code blends bare chassis and finished bodies that are not interchangeable for a given operation. The data also argues for specifying the destination appropriate trim level rather than importing a generic unit and discovering local service cannot support it.

One caveat applies to every figure above: customs statistics record declared export value at the port, not the landed cost a buyer ultimately pays, so freight, insurance, and local duties sit on top of the unit prices shown. The shares and rankings are robust across reporting sources, but the absolute unit prices should be read as relative signals of specification tier rather than firm quotes, and a buyer should always obtain a delivered price for the exact configuration before drawing conclusions from the average.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HS 870421 and which vehicles does it cover?

HS 870421 covers diesel powered goods vehicles with a gross vehicle weight not exceeding 5 tonnes, including chassis cab and box trucks, and in 2024 China exported roughly 518,400 units under this code worth about 6.55 billion US dollars. The category excludes electric and petrol light commercials, so the data captures only diesel units within the 5 tonne limit.

Which country imported the most Chinese HS 870421 trucks in 2024?

Mexico was the largest single destination with about 88,100 units, an 18 percent share of total volume and year on year growth of 14 percent, ahead of Chile at 57,000 units and Vietnam at 49,200 units. No other named market exceeded the 60,000 unit mark in the 2024 aggregate.

What was the average unit price of Chinese light trucks exported under HS 870421?

The average export unit price was approximately 12,600 US dollars, ranging from about 9,800 dollars in price sensitive African markets to 14,500 dollars in Mexico, which reflects higher emissions and equipment specifications. The blended figure mixes bare chassis cab with finished bodies, so actual transaction prices vary widely by configuration.

Which regions showed the fastest growth in Chinese light truck imports?

Vietnam grew 22 percent and South Africa 17 percent year on year, the fastest among major destinations, driven by fleet renewal and distributor network expansion, while Russia fell 4 percent. The broad Others bucket, largely smaller sub Saharan importers, still grew 13 percent.
