What Is the Difference Between FOB, CIF and DAP?

Incoterms determine who pays for what and, just as importantly, where risk transfers from seller to buyer. Confusion here is the most common reason a landed cost blows past budget.

TermWhat the Seller CoversWhat the Buyer CoversBest For
FOBTruck, inland transport to port, export clearance, loadingOcean freight, insurance, destination clearance, duty, inlandBuyers with their own freight forwarder
CIFEverything in FOB plus freight and insurance to destination portDestination clearance, duty, taxes, inland deliveryMost first-time and mid-size importers
DAPEverything in CIF plus inland delivery to a named placeImport duty and taxes, unloadingBuyers without destination logistics capability

None of the three routinely includes import duty or local taxes. That is the buyer's cost in nearly every structure, which is why a landed-cost budget must be built as FOB plus freight plus insurance plus duty plus taxes plus local handling, in that order. Buyers who budget only the FOB number get surprised at the port.

Key point: Always convert a quote to landed cost before comparing it. A USD 50,000 FOB unit and a USD 56,000 CIF unit may be commercially identical once freight and insurance are added, and comparing the headline numbers alone will point you to the wrong supplier.

What Drives Price Variance Between Two SAGMOTO Quotes?

Four variables dominate the outcome.

Engine Choice

The engine is the largest single cost line after the chassis. Within one model, stepping between engine ratings or families can move price by several thousand dollars. The choice should follow the duty cycle: a 430 HP Weichai WP12 for mixed construction work, or a 520 HP Cummins M13 for long-haul work at heavy gross combination weights.

Drive Configuration

A 6x4 costs less than an 8x4 on the same platform because of the additional axle, suspension, brakes and tyres. The 8x4 is worth it when payload and axle-load rules demand it, but an extra axle on a duty cycle that does not need it is wasted capital and tare weight.

Body Type

Chassis-cab pricing is the lowest number you will see. A box, flatbed, stake or hardened steel dump body is fabricated and priced separately. Dump bodies with hydraulic tipping gear are among the most expensive additions, and they are also the most frequently omitted from casual price comparisons.

Spec Level

Climate packages, filtration upgrades, transmission choice, suspension rating, cab trim and safety equipment all add incrementally. Individually they are modest; collectively they add a meaningful percentage to the chassis price. The right test is whether they reduce downtime in your operating environment.

Secondary Drivers

  • Order volume: Larger fleet orders attract better per-unit pricing.
  • Transmission: Automated manual transmissions carry a premium over manuals.
  • Emissions specification: The required emission level for your market affects cost.
  • Currency and raw material movement: Quotes carry a stated validity period for this reason.

Why Do Two Dealers Quote Different Prices for the Same Truck?

Buyers frequently receive two quotes for what appears to be the same truck and find a gap of ten percent or more. The gap is rarely explained by margin alone, and the causes are worth understanding before assuming the cheaper quote is better.

First, the specifications are often not actually the same. One quote may include a tropical cooling package, dual-stage filtration and heavy-duty suspension; the other may be a base specification that looks equivalent on a one-line description. Second, the incoterm may differ: one is FOB, the other CIF to a named port. Third, validity periods differ, and a quote issued months earlier may not reflect current input costs.

Finally, the included scope differs. One dealer may include a parts package, pre-delivery inspection and documentation support; another may list those as extras. The only reliable comparison is a line-itemised quote on an identical written specification, with the same incoterm and validity date.

Key point: Never compare headline prices. Send both dealers the same written specification, request the same incoterm and validity period, and ask for body, options and parts package to be listed as separate line items. The comparison only becomes meaningful at line-item level.

What Are the Standard Deposit, Production and Payment Terms?

Export truck orders follow a fairly standard commercial rhythm, and understanding it helps buyers plan cash flow and delivery timing.

  • Quote validity: Quotes carry a stated validity period; beyond that, input costs may move.
  • Specification freeze: Production scheduling begins once the specification is frozen in writing and the deposit is received.
  • Deposit: Paid at contract signing, with the balance settled before shipment.
  • Payment method: Telegraphic transfer is the norm; letters of credit are used for larger institutional orders.
  • Production window: Indicative production runs several weeks to a few months depending on configuration and order size.

Two practical warnings. Changes requested after production begins are costly and schedule-disruptive, so settle the specification carefully before signing. And because production, transit and clearance can span months, order against a realistic project timeline.

How Should You Compare Two SAGMOTO Quotes?

A disciplined comparison takes about thirty minutes and prevents most expensive mistakes.

  1. Confirm both quotes reference the identical model, engine, drive configuration and transmission.
  2. Confirm both use the same incoterm and name the same port or destination.
  3. Identify whether body fabrication is included, and what the body specification is.
  4. List climate, filtration, suspension and safety packages side by side.
  5. Convert both to landed cost using your own freight, insurance, duty and tax assumptions.
  6. Score the intangibles: parts package, warranty handling, and documentation support.

Buyers who follow this sequence find the apparent price gap narrows substantially, and that the decision then turns on service capability and specification completeness rather than the headline number.

How much does a SAGMOTO heavy dump truck cost FOB in 2026?

A SAGMOTO heavy dump truck such as the X5, X6 or X6s typically quotes in the USD 38,000 to 60,000 FOB range, depending on engine rating, 6x4 or 8x4 drive configuration, and dump body specification. What is the difference between FOB, CIF and DAP pricing?

FOB covers the truck and its loading at the Chinese port only, CIF adds ocean freight and marine insurance to your destination port, and DAP adds inland delivery and import clearance to your named destination. Each step up transfers more cost and risk from the buyer to the seller, and none of the three routinely includes import duty or local taxes.

What drives the price difference between two quotes for the same SAGMOTO model?

Engine choice, drive configuration, body type and spec level are the four dominant drivers, and together they can move a quote by more than USD 10,000 on an otherwise identical model. Order volume, transmission choice and climate packages are the main secondary drivers, and differences in incoterm account for most of the remaining apparent gap.
