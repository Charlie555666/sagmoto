China Dump Truck Export Data 2026: Top 10 Destinations and Regional Growth

2026-10-10Export Data11 min read

Quick answer: in the first eight months of 2026, China’s largest single destination for off-road dump trucks (HS code 870410) by export value was Suriname (US$29.96 million, 12.4% share), followed by Indonesia (US$23.88 million, 9.9%), Russia (US$23.45 million, 9.7%), Mongolia (US$18.21 million, 7.6%) and Brazil (US$11.41 million, 4.7%). On a regional basis Africa is by far the biggest bloc at 31% of export value and +121% year on year, with Indonesia at 19% (+146%). Meanwhile total Chinese heavy-truck exports ran at roughly 40,000 units per month in September 2026 (+21% year on year), on track for about 450,000 units for the full year.

This page consolidates the most reliable publicly reported 2026 dump-truck and heavy-truck export figures, with the source noted for each number — so you can sanity-check any supplier’s claim.

What Counts as a “Dump Truck Export”? Two Different Numbers

Most articles mix two different product categories, which is why the numbers never seem to agree. The distinction matters for buyers:

  • Off-road dump trucks (HS 870410) — purpose-built mining and quarry machines: rigid-frame or wide-body dumpers designed for non-road use. This is the only category with clean, published destination-level customs data.
  • Road-going tippers — a standard 6x4 or 8x4 truck chassis with a tipping body, used on public roads for construction, aggregate and municipal haulage. These are counted inside general truck export statistics (HS 8704 headings), not broken out by body type.

So when you read “China exported 42,000 dump trucks in 2026,” that figure refers to heavy-duty tipper segment estimates — while the customs destination table below covers off-road units specifically. Both are useful; they answer different questions.

Three things stand out. Suriname topping the table is not a typo — it reflects concentrated gold-mining fleet purchases in a very small market. Mongolia’s +99.4% and DR Congo’s +80.9% reflect mining-led fleet expansion. And the appearance of Guinea, Nigeria and Papua New Guinea as new top-tier destinations shows that Chinese off-road dumpers are moving into smaller, resource-driven markets that used to be served almost entirely by used Western equipment.

The pattern is unambiguous: Africa is both the largest and one of the fastest-growing regions for Chinese off-road dump trucks, driven by gold, bauxite, copper and lithium mining cycles plus infrastructure corridors. South America’s -30% headline is misleading — it is a base effect from a large 2025 programme, while Brazil (+16.2%) and Suriname (ranked No.1) continue to grow.

The structural story is that exports are no longer a pressure valve for domestic overcapacity. With close to one in three Chinese commercial vehicles now sold overseas, export markets have become the primary profit engine for the major groups. That is why factories are investing in overseas KD assembly plants, regional parts warehouses and service networks rather than treating export buyers as one-off transactions.

What This Data Means If You Are Buying in 2026

  1. Lead times are lengthening. With export volumes up 30–57% at the two heavy-truck leaders, popular specs (6x4 and 8x4 tippers, 380–430 HP) regularly carry 30–60 day production queues. Book your build slot before your procurement window closes, not after.
  2. Destination data is your negotiating benchmark. If your market appears in the tables above with strong growth, you are not an unusual request — you have leverage, because your specification is standard and your volume fits existing production lines.
  3. High volumes attract grey channels. When a category grows 100%+ in a year, unverified resellers appear. Verify any supplier’s business license on China’s national registry (gsxt.gov.cn) and ask for the factory authorization number — legitimate authorized exporters hold credentials such as SHACMAN authorization No. SQIDACN 28 or SAGMOTO No. SAG-2025-201.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many dump trucks did China export in 2026?
A: It depends on the definition. China Customs data for off-road dump trucks (HS 870410) shows a top-ten destination list led by Suriname, Indonesia and Russia in Jan–Aug 2026. Industry estimates put the heavy-duty tipper segment at roughly 42,000 units for the full year. Total heavy-truck exports are running at about 40,000 units per month.

Q: Which country imports the most Chinese dump trucks?
A: By off-road dump truck export value in Jan–Aug 2026, Suriname ranks first (US$29.96 million), followed by Indonesia, Russia, Mongolia and Brazil. By region, Africa is the largest bloc at 31% of export value.

Q: Where can I verify these dump truck export statistics?
A: China Customs (General Administration of Customs) releases the underlying HS 870410 statistics; industry securities research republishes the regional splits. Total heavy-truck export figures trace to CAAM monthly releases and Sinotruk’s HK-listed interim reports. All 2026 numbers on this page are drawn from those public sources.

Q: Is the South American dump truck market shrinking?
A: The headline -30% for South America in Jan–Apr 2026 is a base effect from a large 2025 programme. Brazil grew +16.2% and Suriname was the single largest destination, so the region is mixed rather than uniformly declining.

Q: How do I know a supplier is a genuine factory-authorized exporter?
A: Check the business license and Unified Social Credit Code on gsxt.gov.cn, verify the brand authorization letter with the factory’s regional office, commission an SGS or BV pre-shipment inspection, and never pay 100% upfront. Our verification guide details each step.
