Southeast Asian Medium-Duty Market Context

The Southeast Asian medium-duty truck market (12-18 tonne GVW) represents approximately 120,000 units annually across Vietnam, Indonesia, the Philippines, Thailand, and Malaysia. Japanese brands (Isuzu, Hino, Mitsubishi Fuso) dominate with a combined 65 percent market share, while Chinese brands have grown from 15 percent in 2020 to 28 percent in 2025, driven by price competitiveness and improving quality perception. The SAGMOTO X7, with its YC4E210 engine and 12-16 tonne GVW range, targets the sweet spot of this market where Japanese trucks are overpriced and underpowered for the growing demand for higher-payload regional distribution. Import Procedures by Country

Vietnam

Vietnam represents the largest medium-duty truck market in Southeast Asia at 35,000-40,000 units annually. Chinese truck imports to Vietnam are well-established through the Huu Nghi (Friendship Pass) border crossing or via Hai Phong port. Import duty on medium-duty trucks (HS code 8704.22) is 20 percent under the ASEAN-China Free Trade Area (ACFTA) agreement, with VAT of 10 percent. The total tax burden is approximately 33 percent of CIF value. Vehicle registration requires a Ministry of Transport type approval certificate and emissions compliance certification.

Indonesia

Indonesia's medium-duty market of 30,000-35,000 units annually offers strong potential for the X7. Import duty under ACFTA is 20 percent with VAT of 11 percent. Indonesian regulations require SNI (Indonesian National Standard) certification for commercial vehicles, including emission testing, safety compliance, and component certification. The SNI certification process takes 6-9 months and costs USD 30,000-50,000. Once obtained, it covers all X7 variants imported under the same certificate.

Philippines

The Philippines imports approximately 15,000-20,000 medium-duty trucks annually. Import duty under ACFTA is 5 percent (the lowest in ASEAN) with VAT of 12 percent. Vehicle registration requires an Import Commodity Clearance (ICC) from the Department of Trade and Industry. The Philippines has the simplest import process in Southeast Asia, with most X7 units clearing customs and registering within 2-3 weeks of vessel arrival at Manila South Harbor.

Thailand

Thailand's medium-duty market of 25,000-30,000 units is dominated by Japanese brands (Isuzu 40 percent, Hino 30 percent, Mitsubishi Fuso 20 percent). Import duty under ACFTA is 20 percent with VAT of 7 percent. Thailand requires Thai Industrial Standard (TIS) certification for commercial vehicles, a process that takes 4-6 months. The Thai market is challenging but rewarding, with fleet operators willing to pay premium prices for trucks with proven reliability and fuel economy.

Body Configuration Options

The X7 chassis is prepared for various body configurations common in Southeast Asian distribution operations:

  • Drop-side flatbed: 7.2-8.5 m length, 600mm side racks, steel floor. Most common body for general cargo distribution. Body cost: USD 2,500-4,000.
  • Box body (curtainside): 7.5-8.0 m length, PVC curtain sides, steel roof and floor. Preferred for FMCG and electronics distribution. Body cost: USD 4,000-6,000.
  • Refrigerated body: 6.5-7.2 m length, 80mm PU insulation, reefer unit (Carrier or Thermo King). For cold chain distribution of food and pharmaceuticals. Body cost: USD 15,000-22,000.
  • Tipper body: 5.5-6.5 m length, 6-8 cubic metre capacity, hydraulic cylinder. For construction material and aggregate transport. Body cost: USD 3,500-5,500.
  • Stake body: 7.0-8.0 m length, 1,200mm removable stakes. For agricultural products and timber transport. Body cost: USD 2,000-3,500.
