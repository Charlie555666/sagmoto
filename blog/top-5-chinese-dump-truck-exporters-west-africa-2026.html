What Are Realistic Prices and Lead Times in 2026?

  • FOB China: 6x4 380-420 HP tipper from roughly US$35,000; 8x4 mining spec from US$48,000 depending on body steel and emission level.
  • CIF West Africa: add approximately US$6,000-9,000 per truck for sea freight and insurance to Lagos/Tema/Abidjan, varying with carrier and season.
  • Production queue: 30-60 days for build-to-order at the major groups in 2026 (Africa demand +66% YoY has tightened slots).
  • Sea transit: 35-50 days Shanghai/Tianjin to West African ports.
  • Total order-to-port: plan 3-4 months; add customs clearance time at destination (Lagos is the slowest of the four main ports).

How Should You Verify an Exporter Before Paying?

West Africa-bound buyers are the most-targeted segment for re-painted used trucks sold as new. Run the same four checks regardless of which of the five brands you choose: (1) verify the exporter's business license and 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code on gsxt.gov.cn; (2) demand the factory authorization letter with a verifiable number — legitimate credentials look like SHACMAN No. SQIDACN 28 or SAGMOTO No. SAG-2025-201 — and confirm it with the factory's regional office; (3) commission an SGS/BV pre-shipment inspection (≈US$300-500 per truck) including VIN and hour-meter verification; (4) pay 30% deposit with balance against B/L copy, never 100% upfront.
