SAGMOTO X6 Product Deep Dive: Engineering, Powertrain and Specifications

October 12, 2026Product Deep Dive15 min read

The SAGMOTO X6 is the backbone of the brand's 16–25-tonne heavy segment — a versatile ladder-frame platform that underpins tippers, mixers, cargo trucks and 6x4 tractors across construction, mining-support and bulk-logistics duty. This deep dive examines the X6's engineering from the frame up: powertrain options, transmission, axles, cab, and the configuration logic that lets one chassis serve so many bodies.

Powertrain Architecture

The X6 is offered with two engine families. The Cummins ISD 6.7 L covers 180–240 HP (700–900 Nm) for lighter tipper and cargo duty, while the Weichai WP10 336–375 HP (1,500–1,650 Nm) covers heavy tipper, mixer and tractor roles. Both meet Euro 5 / E5 emission levels with SCR. The engine mounting uses a three-point hydraulic suspension that isolates cab and frame vibration, extending component life on broken-surface sites.

Transmission and Driveline

Transmission choice follows GVW. The 6x4 tipper uses a FAST 10-speed manual (10JS) rated to 1,800 Nm input, while the 6x4 tractor uses a FAST 12-speed (12JS) with a 2,000 Nm rating and an optional hydraulic retarder for mountain grades. The front axle is a 7.5-tonne forged unit; the rear is a 2 x 13-tonne hub-reduction set on tippers and a 2 x 16-tonne single-reduction set on highway tractors. This split lets the same cab and frame serve both on- and off-road duty.

Frame, Suspension and Brakes

The X6 frame is a 280 x 80 x 8 mm straight ladder (optionally 8+6 mm double-layer for mixer and heavy tipper) with a 3,800–5,000 mm wheelbase on tippers and up to 5,800 mm on cargo. Suspension is multi-leaf front and rear as standard, with a parabolic-option for highway cargo to improve ride and reduce unladen tyre wear. Brakes are full-air S-cam with ABS and optional EBS; a 400 L air reservoir supports frequent stop-start on sites.

Engineering insight: The X6's genius is commonality — one cab, one frame family, and a single FAST driveline envelope cover tipper, mixer, cargo and tractor roles. That commonality is what lets Fenghan Trade hold a deep parts inventory and offer short lead times for every X6 body type.

Cab and Driver Environment

The X6 shares SAGMOTO's wide-body composite cab (2,300 mm) with a flat floor, air-suspended seat, hydraulic tilt, and a 7-inch digital cluster. The sleeper option adds a 600 mm bunk for two-driver bulk-haul and mixer relay duties. Cab corrosion protection uses a 9-stage pre-treatment and electrophoretic primer rated for coastal and high-salt mining environments.

Configuration Logic by Application

For a 6x4 20-tonne tipper, specify the WP10.336, 10JS, 13-tonne hub-reduction rear, and 8+6 mm frame. For an 8 m³ mixer, specify the WP10.375, 10JS with PTO, and reinforced front to carry the drum overhang. For a 6x4 tractor (GCW 60t), specify the WP10.375, 12JS with retarder, and 16-tonne single-reduction rear. The SAGMOTO dump truck models 6x4 8x4 guide and the SAGMOTO tractor trucks prime mover range build directly on this logic.

Conclusion

The SAGMOTO X6 is a genuinely platform-level product: a single well-engineered chassis that becomes a tipper, mixer, cargo truck or tractor through specification rather than redesign. With Cummins and Weichai power, a FAST driveline, and a cab built for harsh sites, it offers European-style versatility at a Chinese price.
