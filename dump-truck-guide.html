2. X3000 / X3s — The Heavy Duty Benchmark

The X3000 platform is the truck that built SAGMOTO's export reputation. Powered by the Cummins ISME family (345 / 420 HP) with the Fast 12JSD200T 12-speed transmission, it is available as a 6x4 construction tipper and an 8x4 mining tipper with a reinforced frame and Hardi-type suspension options.

  • Proven powertrain — Cummins ISME 420 with Westport-compatible fuel calibration for high-sulphur fuels in several African and Central Asian markets.
  • Frame — double-layer 8+8 mm stiffened rails on 8x4 mining variants; optional 10 mm for abrasive overloads.
  • Axles — 13 t Hande HD front / 16 t tandem rear with differential locks on mining spec.
  • Emissions — Euro II / Euro III / Euro IV configurable, plus LHD / RHD.

3. E3 — The 25-Tonne Construction Specialist

The E3 6x4 dump truck pairs the Yuchai YC6MK 400 HP with the Fast 12JSD200T — a lower-cost alternative to the Cummins route with excellent parts availability in Africa. Typical payloads reach 25 t with a 16 m3 hardened body. It is the reference truck for construction fleets in West Africa, the Philippines and Central Asia. 4. X6 & X9 — Medium and Light Duty Tippers

Not every job needs 420 HP. The X6 (Cummins ISD 210 HP, Fast 10JSD180) covers county-road and township projects; the X9 4x2 (Yuchai 140-160 HP) is ideal for city renovation and small quarry runs where fuel economy and low purchase price dominate. Both share SAGMOTO's cab safety cell and the same export documentation. Watch the X6 vs X6s dump comparison for the differences between generations.

8. FAQ

How much does a SAGMOTO dump truck cost FOB China?

Indicative 2026 FOB prices: X9 4x2 from USD 18,000; X6 6x4 around USD 30,000; X3s 6x4 USD 38,000-45,000; 8x4 mining USD 48,000-55,000. Request a formal quote with your configuration.

What is the difference between 6x4 and 8x4?

6x4 has one rear tandem axle for general construction (25-40 t GVW); 8x4 has two rear axles with higher legal payload and stability for mining — the X3000 8x4 is the classic mining configuration.

Which model is best for mining?

The X3000/X3s 8x4 with Cummins ISME 420 HP for most mines, or the E1st with Cummins Z14 560 HP where gradients and GCW are extreme. The E3 6x4 with YC6MK 400 HP is a proven mid-size quarry truck.

What body volumes are available?

From 8-10 m3 light bodies to 16 m3 construction bodies and 20-30 m3 U-shape mining bodies with NM400 wear liners.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship to 50+ countries — RoRo/flat-rack for single units, bulk and CKD for fleets. West Africa 40-55 days, East Africa 30-40 days, Middle East 25-35 days, Central Asia rail 18-25 days.
