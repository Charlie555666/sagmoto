How to Verify Any Chinese Truck Exporter in 4 Steps

  1. Registry check. Enter the company’s Unified Social Credit Code on China’s national enterprise registry (gsxt.gov.cn) and confirm the legal name, registration date and business scope. Ours is 91610104MACWWH682Q.
  2. Authorization check. Ask for the factory authorization letter, then call or email the brand’s regional export office and quote the authorization number (SQIDACN 28 or SAG-2025-201 for us). A number the factory cannot confirm is a red flag.
  3. Independent inspection. Commission an SGS or BV pre-shipment inspection — typically US$300–500 per truck — covering VIN, build sheet, mileage (for used units) and rust treatment.
  4. Payment structure. Never pay 100% upfront. A 30% deposit with balance against B/L copy is the industry norm; a supplier who insists on full prepayment is a risk regardless of brand.
