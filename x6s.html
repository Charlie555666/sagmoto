X6s Series

Premium Comfort Meets Proven Durability

X6s Series

The SAGMOTO X6s is the premium upgrade of the X6 medium-duty platform, manufactured by Shaanxi Automobile Group Commercial Vehicle Co., Ltd. and exported under the SAGMOTO brand. Built on the same proven chassis architecture as the X6, the X6s adds the M3000 semi-high roof cab with sedan-like interior comfort, maintenance-free high-efficiency drive axles, and upgraded trim — making it the preferred choice for fleets running extended shift cycles in construction, mining support, and regional distribution. Power comes from Cummins ISD engines rated 180-240 HP (Yuchai YC4D/YC4E options for cost-sensitive markets), paired with a Fast 10-speed manual transmission. Available in 4×2, 6×4 and 4×4 all-wheel-drive configurations, the X6s has been delivered worldwide as dump trucks, fuel tankers, compressed garbage trucks, wreckers, cargo trucks and crane carriers — including recent shipments to Kyrgyzstan, Armenia and Costa Rica. The reinforced Q690 high-tensile steel frame, Hande axles and low-rolling-resistance tires deliver the durability and fuel economy that SAGMOTO is known for, while genuine OEM parts supply through Shaanxi Fenghan Trading keeps total cost of ownership low across Africa, Central Asia, the Middle East and Latin America.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between X6 and X6s?

The X6s is the premium upgrade of the X6 medium-duty platform. It features the M3000 semi-high roof cab with sedan-like interior comfort, maintenance-free high-efficiency drive axles, and upgraded trim. Both share the same chassis architecture and Cummins ISD / Yuchai powertrain options.

What engine options are available for the X6s?

The X6s offers Cummins ISD engines (180-240 HP) for international markets and Yuchai YC4D/YC4E options for cost-sensitive regions, paired with a Fast 10-speed manual transmission. Emission compliance covers EURO II, EURO III and EURO V.

What configurations does the X6s come in?

The X6s is available in 4×2, 6×4 and 4×4 all-wheel-drive configurations. Popular body variants include 6×4 dump truck, 6×4 fuel tanker, 6×4 garbage compactor, 4×2 wrecker, cargo truck and truck-mounted crane.

What is the payload capacity of the X6s dump truck?

The X6s 6×4 dump truck is rated for 15-25 tons payload depending on body specification, within a GVWR range of 12T to 60T (GCW). The reinforced Q690 high-tensile frame and Hande axles are sized for construction and mining duty cycles.

Can the X6s be customized for specialized applications?

Yes. X6s chassis have been delivered as fuel tankers, compressed garbage trucks, wreckers, knuckle-boom crane carriers and cargo boxes for markets including Kyrgyzstan, Armenia and Costa Rica. Shaanxi Fenghan Trading works with certified body builders for compliant installations.
