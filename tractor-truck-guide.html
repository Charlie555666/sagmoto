2. X3000 / X3s — The Long-Haul Benchmark

The X3000 6x4 with Cummins ISME 420 HP and the Fast 12JSD200T 12-speed is the volume tractor for cross-border logistics: East African corridors, Central Asian railways feeding, Middle East port drayage. With 400-500 L dual tanks, JOST fifth wheel and optional air suspension, it delivers European-brand duty cycles at roughly half the purchase price and with far cheaper parts.

  • Engine options — Cummins ISME 345/420 (premium) or Yuchai YC6MK 400 (value route).
  • Axles — 13 t front / 16 t rear tandem, diff lock optional for mountain routes.
  • Cab — flat-floor sleeper with 2-berth, AC double evaporator for tropical markets.
  • Deep dives — X3s 6x4 tractor deep dive, Central Asia buying guide.

3. E1st & Z3 — Heavy Haul Flagships

Where GCW exceeds 60 t — mining concentrate, steel coil, timber — the flagship pair takes over. The E1st matches the Cummins Z14 560 HP with the Fast 16JSD240T 16-speed; the Z3 pairs the Cummins M13 520 HP with the 12JSD240TA. Both offer air suspension cabs, 600 L+ fuel capacity and 16 t reinforced axles. Comparisons: Z3 vs KamAZ-65206, Z3 vs Scania R500, Z3 vs Volvo FH16, E1st vs FAW J7.

4. X6 & X9 — Regional and Light Tractors

For distribution radii under 400 km, the X6 (Cummins ISD 210 HP) and the X9 4x2 (Yuchai 140-160 HP) offer the lowest total cost per kilometre in the SAGMOTO range — day cab, 6-speed, and the same export documentation and parts network as the flagships.

The X3000/X3s 6x4 with Cummins ISME 420 HP covers 40-60 t GCW corridors. Above 60 t, move to the Z3 (M13 520) or E1st (Z14 560).

How much does a SAGMOTO tractor cost?

FOB Xi'an: X9 4x2 from USD 17,000; X6 USD 26-30k; X3000 6x4 USD 36-44k; Z3/E1st flagships USD 55-70k depending on spec.

What fifth wheel options?

Yes — RHD is available across the range for East/Southern Africa and other right-hand markets at the same lead time.
