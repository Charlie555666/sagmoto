1. Shacman vs SAGMOTO — Same Truck, Two Names

Shacman is the domestic brand of Shaanxi Automobile Group; SAGMOTO is its export brand. The X3000 you buy as SAGMOTO comes from the same Xi'an production lines, with export-specific calibration: LHD or RHD, Euro II to Euro VI emissions, tropical cooling packages, and documentation packs for destination markets. You are not buying a "copy" — you are buying the original truck with an export nameplate.

3. Powertrain: Cummins ISME + Fast 12JSD200T

The heart of the export X3000 is the Cummins ISME 345/420 HP mated to the Fast 12JSD200T 12-speed — a combination proven over hundreds of millions of kilometres across African and Middle Eastern fleets. The alternative Yuchai YC6MK 400 route lowers purchase cost and matches local parts familiarity in several markets.

  • Cooling — tropical-validated radiators for 45C+ ambient operation.
  • Fuel tolerance — calibration options for high-sulphur diesel markets.
  • Driveline — 13 t front / 16 t tandem rear axles; diff lock on mining spec.
  • Emissions — Euro II / III / IV selectable per destination regulation.

4. Configurations: Tractor, Dump, Cargo

Tractor — JOST 50/90 fifth wheel, sleeper cab, 400-500 L tanks: the corridor workhorse. Dump — 6x4 with 14-16 m3 construction body, 8x4 with 20-30 m3 U-shape mining body on a reinforced frame. Cargo — box, curtain-side or flatbed for palletised freight. Full detail in the tractor guide and dump truck guide.
